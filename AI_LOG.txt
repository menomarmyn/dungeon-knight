AI LOG — Midterm Project "Dungeon Knight"
Tool: Claude (Anthropic), used as a coding assistant.

WHAT I ASKED THE AI TO DO
  - Turn my A4–A7 project (Player2D, Health, Shooter, Bullet, Hazard, Spawner, CameraFollow2D) into a full
    5-screen mobile platformer in a Soul Knight-like pixel style.
  - Generate pixel-art sprites, sounds and music (procedurally, so there are no license problems).
  - Write an editor script (Midterm -> Build Everything) that creates scenes, prefabs, animators and the HUD.

WHAT THE AI PRODUCED
  - Editor tool: Assets/Midterm/Editor/MidtermBuilder.cs (scene/prefab/animator generation — not game logic).
  - Updated my scripts: Player2D (Input System, ground check, coyote time, moving platforms),
    Health (hearts HUD, knockback, death -> restart), Shooter (Fire action), Bullet (now inherits Projectile),
    Hazard (breaks on the ground), CameraFollow2D (level bounds, shake).
  - New scripts: Projectile, EnemyProjectile, Enemy, SlimeEnemy, BatEnemy, Boss, Pickup, Crystal, HeartPickup,
    Spikes, ExitPortal, MovingPlatform, HUD, GameAudio, SceneFader, MenuButtons, SafeArea, Parallax,
    AutoDestroy, PressFeedback, FloatingDust.
  - Art, audio and level text maps.

PROBLEMS WE HIT AND HOW THEY WERE FIXED
  1. Compile error CS0826 (implicitly-typed Color/Color32 array) in the builder -> explicit Color[] array.
  2. Deprecated FindObjectsByType(FindObjectsSortMode) warning -> the overload without the sort mode.
  3. The hero fell through the floor and the level restarted in a loop: the CompositeCollider2D of the tilemap
     was saved empty because tiles were placed from an editor script.
     Fix: TilemapCollider2D.ProcessTilemapChanges() + CompositeCollider2D.GenerateGeometry() after placing tiles.
  4. The on-screen joystick creates a virtual gamepad; to be sure PlayerInput receives it, Player2D pairs
     all devices with the player (InputUser.PerformPairingWithDevice).

WHAT I DID / CHECKED MYSELF
  - Chose the concept: 2D pixel action platformer in the Soul Knight style, 5 screens, boss at the end.
  - Decided to keep my A4–A7 logic and physics and asked mainly for visual changes, so my old scripts stayed the base.
  - Ran Build Everything in Unity 6.6 and played every level in the Editor; reported the bugs I found
    (hero falling through the floor, title cut off on the tablet, hero hidden under the joystick at spawn).
  - Tested on my iPhone with Unity Remote 5: the picture was blurry, then the game lagged and the touch
    controls did not work; I reported each problem and checked the fix again on the phone.
  - Recorded and presented the defense video myself.

I understand that code written with AI help is graded at most 90%, and that I must be able to explain every line.
