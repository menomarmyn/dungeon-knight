    AutoDestroy, PressFeedback, FloatingDust.
  - Art, audio and level text maps.

PROBLEMS WE HIT AND HOW THEY WERE FIXED
  1. Compile error CS0826 (implicitly-typed Color/Color32 array) in the builder -> explicit Color[] array.
  2. Deprecated FindObjectsByType(FindObjectsSortMode) warning -> the overload without the sort mode.
  3. The hero fell through the floor and the level restarted in a loop: the CompositeCollider2D of the tilemap
     was saved empty because tiles were placed from an editor script.
     Fix: TilemapCollider2D.ProcessTilemapChanges() + CompositeCollider2D.GenerateGeometry() after placing tiles.
  4. The on-screen joystick creates a virtual gamepad; to be sure PlayerInput receives it, Player2D pairs
     all devices with the player (InputUser.PerformPairingWithDevice).

  - Ran Build Everything in Unity 6.6 and played every level in the Editor; reported the bugs I found

  - Tested on my iPhone with Unity Remote 5: the picture was blurry, then the game lagged and the touch

WEEK 12 · SOUND PASS
======================================================================
See DECISIONS.md (week 12) for the decision log.
AI produced: GameAudio.cs (rewritten: mixer groups, pool of sources, pitch and clip variation), MusicManager.cs
(one instance, 1 s crossfade, pause snapshot), AudioPrefs.cs and VolumeSlider.cs (dB, PlayerPrefs), changes in

The AI also created the GameMix AudioMixer in the Unity editor on my laptop (computer control) and rebuilt the game.
WHAT I DID MYSELF
Listened to every sound on my phone and laptop and changed: the rock rumble was too loud and masked the boss wind-up, so I lowered it by about 4 dB; the lose sound felt too harsh on phone speakers, so I picked a softer clip and slightly lowered its pitch variation; I shortened the menu music crossfade from 1 s to 0.6 s because it felt sluggish; and I rebalanced music vs. SFX (music −6 dB, SFX 0 dB) after testing with headphones.
Checked the settings test on the phone: changed the volume, closed the app, opened it again. The slider position and volume were restored correctly via PlayerPrefs; tested mute → unmute and confirmed the pause snapshot still applies while paused, then fixed one case where the slider showed the wrong value after reopening.
Made the week 12 commits in GitHub Desktop (separate commits for audio scripts, generated sounds, builder/settings changes) and recorded the video: settings window, volume persistence after restart, boss wind-up and rock rumble in gameplay, and the music crossfade between menu and game.
Additional things I did myself
Fixed a bug where the source pool ran out during heavy rock spawns (several rumble sounds cut off) — increased the pool size and added a "steal oldest" fallback in GameAudio.
Noticed the pitch variation on repeated SFX (spawning, hits) sounded "wobbly" when spam-clicked, so I narrowed the pitch range from ±0.15 to ±0.08.
Tested the game with headphones, phone speaker, and muted — checked that mute fully silences both music and SFX and that the game doesn't crash or leak audio sources when muted.
Tuned the boss wind-up so it's clearly audible over the music but doesn't clip; added a small delay so it doesn't overlap the rock rumble.
Verified the mixer groups in the editor: Master → Music / SFX, confirmed the pause snapshot ducks music but not UI sounds, and re-saved the GameMix asset after the AI's edits.
Re-imported the 12 generated sounds with mobile import settings (Vorbis, load in background, mono for SFX) and checked file sizes — trimmed a few that were unnecessarily long.
Playtested a full run on the phone: no audio dropouts, no pops/clicks at crossfade or on pause, volume slider responds smoothly across the whole range.
Updated DECISIONS.md (week 12) with my own notes on the loudness/pitch decisions and the pool-size fix, and added a short note in the sound script map where the map didn't match the final clip assignment.
Cleaned up: removed unused audio clips from the project, checked that no missing-reference warnings appear in the console on a fresh scene load.
